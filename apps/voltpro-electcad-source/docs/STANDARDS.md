# Standards and engineering scope

VoltPro is an open engineering-workbench project. It is not certified and does not currently claim compliance with any IEC, EN, DIN or VDE standard.

## Intended future review areas
- IEC 60617: graphical symbols for diagrams.
- IEC 61082: preparation of electrotechnical documents.
- IEC 81346: reference designation systems.
- IEC 60204-1: electrical equipment of machinery.
- Applicable DIN EN and VDE requirements, selected according to actual product scope and jurisdiction.

The list is an investigation plan, not a claim that all listed standards apply to every drawing. Obtain current authoritative editions and qualified review before implementing normative rules. Do not copy restricted standard text or symbol databases without appropriate rights.

## Current status
- The editor has an original, generic symbol renderer and a starter open component catalogue.
- Component references can be auto-numbered by catalogue prefix.
- No authoritative standard-derived symbol validation, automatic wire numbering, coil/contact cross-reference engine, terminal strip report, certified title-block templates, or DIN/VDE rule engine exists.
- A component symbol or successful DC solve is not proof of standards compliance or electrical safety.
