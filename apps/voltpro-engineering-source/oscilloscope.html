VoltPro Oscilloscope

Simulation generates sampled signals; this browser does not measure external voltages. CH1 yellow, CH2 cyan. A physical device requires a separately supported acquisition adapter.

The viewport is 10 time divisions by 8 voltage divisions. Channel positions move the zero references, not the measured signal. Scale and pan only change the view. Wheel zooms; Shift+wheel pans. Arrow keys pan. Double-click resets the view.

Simulation: ±10 V range, selectable 8/12/16-bit quantization. Ideal unlimited analog bandwidth; no analog anti-alias filter. AC coupling uses a first-order 10 Hz high-pass with a 150 ms warm-up. GND is zero reference. Noise is deterministic for reproducibility.

Measurements use the complete captured record, after coupling and quantization. Frequency uses averaged rising midpoint crossings; duty and pulse width use midpoint thresholds. Rise/fall times use 10–90% thresholds and require at least three transition samples. Unavailable results are explained.

CSV requires time_s,CH1_V,CH2_V, seconds and volts, increasing uniform timestamps (0.1% tolerance), 3–100,000 samples. Lines beginning # are metadata. Export preserves all captured samples and settings.

Auto trigger captures without an edge after 500 ms. Normal waits and retains the previous record. Single always waits for a valid edge, captures once, and stops. Hardware adapters, FFT, XY, and persistence are future extensions; no external hardware is connected.
