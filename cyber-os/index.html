6workflow phases

01 / reconnaissance workflowpassive → active
01ScopeWrite the authorised domain, IP range, application or organisation boundary. Record exclusions.
02DiscoverCollect public names, certificates, DNS, indexed URLs, historical assets and organisation signals.
03CorrelateJoin subdomains, IPs, ASNs, certificates, technologies and historical records. Expect gaps.
04ResolveConfirm which discovered names currently resolve and identify CNAME, A, AAAA, MX and NS relationships.
05InspectFor authorised targets, inspect HTTP metadata, technologies, pages, screenshots and application entry points.
06ValidateManually verify important findings. Preserve source, timestamp and evidence before calling something a finding.
SAFETY BOUNDARY Passive OSINT can still expose personal or sensitive information. Minimise collection, respect terms and privacy, and do not pivot into intrusive testing unless the target and action are explicitly authorised.
